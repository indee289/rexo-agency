About Rexo Agency

Rexo Agency is a full-service influencer marketing agency founded by Raj Kandiya and based in the heart of Jodhpur, Rajasthan. Our mission is to revolutionize how brands communicate with their audiences by leveraging the power of authentic influencer connections. We believe that real influence comes from genuine storytelling and targeted reach.

With a strong network of influencers across various niches such as fashion, beauty, fitness, tech, travel, lifestyle, and more, we help businesses of all sizes tap into new markets and grow their brand visibility.

We develop customized strategies, manage your campaigns from start to finish, and ensure measurable results. Whether you’re a startup or an established brand, we provide the expertise and support to make your campaign successful.
